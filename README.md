Clemir AI

AI-Powered Audience Simulation, Quantitative Forecasting & Consumer Intelligence Platform

Clemir AI is an intelligent decision-support platform that helps founders, product teams, marketers, and researchers validate business ideas, understand customer behavior, analyze market opportunities, and forecast future trends — before investing real-world resources.

By combining multi-persona AI simulations, TimesFM-inspired time-series forecasting, real-time web research, and an interactive AI research assistant, Clemir AI transforms complex business decisions into structured, evidence-backed insights.

🌟 Overview

Traditional market research can be expensive, time-consuming, and difficult to scale. Clemir AI addresses these challenges through AI-powered audience intelligence and quantitative analysis.

Users can define a product concept, pricing strategy, A/B experiment, usability flow, or market scenario, then simulate how different customer segments might respond.

The platform generates:

AI-powered audience responses from diverse customer personas.

Structured sentiment analysis and behavioral insights.

Persona-level questions and answers.

Evidence-backed findings with source references.

Quantitative P10, P50, and P90 forecasting projections.

Historical forecasting validation and performance metrics.

Interactive AI-assisted research and decision support.

🔄 How Clemir AI Works

The platform follows a structured research and decision-support workflow.

Define the Research Objective
Select a simulation mode and describe the product, business idea, pricing strategy, or market scenario.

Configure the Audience
Select behavioral personas, define audience segments, and configure AI agent counts.

Provide Additional Context
Attach supporting documents, URLs, historical metrics, or custom research information.

Run Audience Simulations
Generate persona-level responses, behavioral insights, sentiment scores, and structured research findings.

Analyze Quantitative Forecasts
Process historical time-series data and generate P10, P50, and P90 projections with backtesting metrics.

Ground Findings with Web Research
Retrieve external information and associate relevant evidence with research conclusions.

Explore Results with the AI Assistant
Ask follow-up questions, investigate individual personas, and explore research findings.

Export Research Reports
Download results in PDF, JSON, CSV, or TXT formats.

⚠️ Research Disclaimer

Clemir AI provides AI-generated audience simulations and analytical forecasts for research and decision-support purposes.

Simulated persona responses are not equivalent to responses collected from real consumers or statistically representative market surveys. Forecasts are estimates based on available historical data and modeling assumptions, not guarantees of future outcomes.

External research evidence should be independently evaluated before making consequential business decisions.
